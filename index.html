A voice built over a lifetime

James has sung in public since he was six years old. From church stages to working bands, he has performed professionally for more than three decades, with a deep baritone and a stage presence that fills a room.

Today he brings that experience to events of every size. He sings live, hosts karaoke for adult and family-style crowds, and runs trivia and bingo nights. He has hosted weekly shows for four years, so he knows how to keep a room engaged.

He is always working to become a better artist, and he talks through every event with the host first so he arrives prepared.
